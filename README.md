# Supermarket-Sales-Report
An interactive dashboard uncovering sales performance. Tracks revenue, gross income, customer behavior and product trends across 3 cities. Identifies peak hours and top-performing branches to help management optimize staffing, stock and retention strategies.

# Project Overview 
These captures detailed transaction records from a supermarket chain in Myanmar with branches in Yangon, Mandalay and Naypyitaw. Over the last year, the company tracked sales across product categories, customer demographics and locations. The goal is to optimize sales performance, improve customer experience, and drive business growth by understanding revenue trends and customer behavior, identifying top-preforming products, branches and customer segments and informing data-driven decisions to boost sales, profit, and customer loyalty.

# Problem Statement 
The supermarket chain in Myanmar has 12 months of sales data but lacks clear insights into what's driving performance. Management need to understand the revenue performance, customer behavior, product + payment trends, and operational patterns.

# Data Collection and Description
The dataset used in this project was gotten from kaggle. Based on the data dictionary, key fields include:
1. Invoice ID: Unique identifier for each transaction.
2. Branch: The branch location of the supermarket (e.g., Yangon, Naypyitaw, Mandalay).
3. City: The city in which the supermarket branch is located.
4. Customer Type: Indicates whether the customer is a ‘Member’ or ‘Normal’.
5. Gender: Gender of the customer.
6. Product Line: The category of the product sold (e.g., Health & Beauty, Electronic Accessories, Home & Lifestyle).
7. Unit Price: Price per unit of the product.
8. Quantity: Number of items purchased.
9. Tax 5%: Calculated tax amount on the transaction at a 5% rate.
10. Total: Total amount for the transaction including tax.
11. Date: Date of the transaction.
12. Time: Time of the transaction.
13. Payment: Payment method used (e.g., Cash, Ewallet, Credit card).
14. COGS: Cost of goods sold, representing the raw cost of the products.
15. Gross Margin Percentage: Fixed percentage of profit for each sale (4.7619%).
16. Gross Income: Profit earned from the transaction.
17. Rating: Customer satisfaction rating (out of 10).

# Dashboard Insight
The interactive dashboard in PowerBi was designed to present insights clearly and efficiently. ur analysis on the Supermarket Sales Report above revealed some exciting insights:
1. January generates the highest revenue, with a significant spike in sales.
2. Napytaw is the top-performing city, generating more revenue than Mandalay and Yangon.
3. Food and Beverages is the most popular product line in Napytaw that genrates higher revenue, followed by Sports and Travel, Electronics, and Fashinon and Accessories.
4. Oldies Customers are the primary drivers of revenue becase the oldies thinks more on foods and beverage to sports and travel, electronics, fashion and accessories, followed by the Gen-z who thinks the reverse of the oldies.
5. Female customers generate more revenue for the supermarket.
6. Evening hours(4pm — 9pm) generates the highest sales, with revenue increasing during this period.
7. Cash is the preferred payment method among customers.
8. Weekends and evening periods show a significant increase in sales and order.

# Recommendations
1. Reward members who are customers: offer loyalty rewards and discounts to members to encourage repeat purchases and attract new customers.
